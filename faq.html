FAQ

Good questions.

Getting started

What happens at the meet-and-greet?

A relaxed first meeting before any walk. Your dog gets to know me, and we go over routines, lead habits, triggers, feeding times and how you would like updates.

What should I have ready?

A well-fitting lead and collar or harness with an ID tag, plus any treats or poo bags you prefer. Please make sure vaccinations are up to date and your dog is microchipped and registered, as required in NSW.

Which areas do you cover?

North Kellyville and nearby suburbs. If you're just outside the area, send a request and I'll let you know.

How do I get in touch?

For now, use the booking form, or the quote form on the Home help or Car care page. Other contact options are coming soon.

Your dog

My dog is anxious, a rescue or reactive. Can you help?

Please mention it in the booking form. We'll take it slowly, starting with the meet-and-greet, and plan quieter routes and times that suit your dog.

Do you walk puppies and senior dogs?

Yes. Walks are kept short and gentle for puppies and older dogs, at a pace that suits them. Let me know their age and any health needs.

Can you walk more than one dog?

Yes, up to 3 dogs from the same home can walk together: $30 for 2 dogs or $50 for 3. For 4 or more dogs, please mention it in the notes.

How long are the walks?

Each walk is one hour.

Walks and updates

How will I know how the walk went?

You'll get a short message after each walk letting you know how it went.

What about rain, heat or storms?

Light rain is fine and walks go ahead. In storms or extreme heat, I'll message you to reschedule or suggest a shorter, cooler walk.

What if my dog seems unwell or there's an emergency?

If your dog seems unwell before a walk, the walk won't go ahead and I'll let you know. In an emergency, I'll contact you straight away. We'll agree an emergency plan, including your preferred vet, at the meet-and-greet.

How do you handle keys and house access?

We'll agree on a method at the meet-and-greet. Please don't send keys, alarm codes or lockbox details through the booking form.

Bookings and payment

How much does it cost?

A one hour walk is $20 for 1 dog, $30 for 2 dogs, or $50 for 3 dogs walked together. You'll see the price before you send your booking.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 4pm to 6pm. Walks are one hour, starting at 4pm or 5pm.

Do you walk on Sundays?

No, there are no walks on Sundays. Please choose Monday to Saturday.

How do cancellations and rescheduling work?

Just let me know as early as you can and we'll find another time. A full cancellation policy is coming soon.

Do you walk during school holidays and public holidays?

Holiday availability is coming soon. Send a request with your dates and I'll let you know.

How do I pay?

Nothing is paid online. Payment details are coming soon and will be sorted out once your first walk is confirmed.

Are you insured?

Details about insurance and safety are coming soon. Feel free to ask at the meet-and-greet.

Home help and car care

How do I get a quote for home help or car care?

Fill in the quote form on the Home help or Car care page. I'll get back to you with a quote before any work is booked.

How much do home help and car care cost?

Every job is different, so it's a free quote on request. You'll know the price before anything is agreed.

Do you do engine or mechanical work?

No. Car care is cleaning only: hand wash, interior vacuum and wipe-down, windows and tyre shine. No engine bay or mechanical work.

What home help jobs do you do?

Ground-level jobs like lawn mowing, weeding, garden tidy-ups, hedge trimming, bin washing and small odd jobs. No roof, gutter, electrical or plumbing work.
