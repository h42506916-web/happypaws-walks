FAQ

Good questions.

Which areas do you cover?

North Kellyville and nearby streets. If you're just outside the area, send a request and I'll let you know.

How long are the walks?

Choose a 30-minute stroll or a 60-minute adventure through local parks.

Do you walk more than one dog?

Yes, dogs from the same home can walk together. Let me know in the booking notes.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine, and walks go ahead. In storms or extreme heat, I'll message you to reschedule.

How do I pay?

Nothing is paid online. We'll sort out the details once your first walk is confirmed.
