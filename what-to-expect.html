What to expect

Simple from the first hello.

New to dog walkers? Here is how it works, step by step.

  1. Send a request

    Use the booking form to tell me about your dog and the times that suit you.

  2. Meet-and-greet

    A relaxed first meeting so your dog can get to know me, and we can go over routines, lead habits and any quirks.

  3. First walk

    We agree on a time, route length and how I collect and return your dog.

  4. Update after every walk

    A short message after each walk letting you know how it went.
