Car care

How much does it cost?

There are three packages: Quick Clean $25 (exterior hand wash and windows), Full Clean $60 (adds interior vacuum, wipe-down and tyre shine) and Showroom $100 (adds wheel clean, door jambs, dash and console detail and glass polish). Choose one on the Book page.

What's included?

Exterior hand wash, interior vacuum, interior wipe-down, windows inside and out, tyre shine and wheel clean, done in your driveway at home.

Do you do engine or mechanical work?

No. It's cleaning only. I don't clean engine bays or do any engine, mechanical, servicing, repair or paint correction work.

What if it rains?

If it rains I'll get in touch and we'll move your clean to another day.

Who does the work?

I do. I'm Harvey, a local in North Kellyville, and Happy Paws is my small neighbourhood business.

Do I need to be home?

The car needs to be somewhere I can reach it, like your driveway. Mention anything I should know in the notes, such as where to find a tap.

How do I cancel?

Open the Calendar page on the device you booked with and tap Cancel. I'll get an email straight away.

How do I pay?

Nothing is paid online. Payment details are coming soon and will be sorted out when I confirm your booking.

Which areas do you cover?

North Kellyville and nearby suburbs. If you're just outside the area, send a request and I'll let you know.
